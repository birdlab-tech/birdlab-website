Every few months, BirdLab hosts technical presentations, seminars and other industry events in collaboration with academic institutions and commercial partners. Individuals with an interest in BirdLab's work are welcome to register their interest by completing the enquiry form below.

Invitations are issued at the organiser's discretion according to the nature and capacity of each event.

Please note that BirdLab does not engage with unsolicited sales approaches through this channel. Where specialist products or services are required, suppliers are selected following an active search and procurement process.

Selecting “SEND ENQUIRY” opens your own email application with a drafted message. This website does not store or submit these form fields to a server. Your email will only be sent when you choose to send it from your email application. See the Privacy Notice for information about how BirdLab uses enquiries.
